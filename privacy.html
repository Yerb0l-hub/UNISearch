Privacy and data use

UNISearch is a pilot education tool. Your student profile and saved shortlist are stored in your browser's local storage. There is no account system or synced database in this MVP.

When you ask a question in the AI Advisor, only the question and a limited set of profile fields (academic scores, intended major, preferred countries, tuition budget and scholarship preference) are submitted to the server. Free-text activities are not sent automatically. If real AI is configured, that information is sent through Vercel AI Gateway to an AI provider to generate the reply. If the AI service is unavailable, advice is generated locally.

Do not share passport numbers, home addresses, passwords or other identifying information. Do not rely on AI responses for admission eligibility or scholarship awards. Clear your local profile using the Profile page, and clear your browser website data to remove the saved shortlist.

Before public release, the founder must establish a contact address, privacy-policy owner, data retention policy, an appropriate privacy compliance review, and robust abuse controls for any publicly accessible paid AI endpoint.
